I'm the one person behind the ohgeeceee network. Based in Montana, I build the things I couldn't find: explainers with no jargon, diagnostics with no dealer, public records with no gatekeeping, and tools for the communities I actually live in.

Every site here is free, ad-free, and tracker-free. Open-source where it can be. No growth hacks, no engagement tricks — just small, sharp software that respects the person using it. And when your own tech breaks, I fix that too — remote tech support, plain answers, no jargon.
